# Pace Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pace handles information.

## Summary

Pace is designed to be privacy-first:

- No account creation
- No ads
- No tracking
- No third-party analytics SDKs
- No selling or sharing of personal data

## Data Pace Uses

Pace may request access to:

- **Apple Health data (read only):**
  - Steps
  - Walking + Running Distance
- **Motion & Fitness:**
  - Used for live step updates while the app is open
- **Notifications (optional):**
  - Used for daily goal-achievement alerts

## How Data Is Used

Pace uses this data only to:

- Show your daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly step summaries
- Display charts and progress toward your goal
- Provide optional live updates and notifications

## Data Storage

- Data shown in Pace is stored locally on your device.
- Pace does not require a remote account.
- Pace does not upload your health data to our servers.

## Data Sharing

Pace does **not** sell, rent, or share your personal health data with third parties for advertising or profiling.

## Your Controls

You can change permissions any time:

1. Open iOS **Settings -> Apps -> Pace**
2. Open Apple **Health -> Profile -> Apps -> Pace**
3. Adjust Health and Motion & Fitness permissions

You can also remove the app at any time.

## Children’s Privacy

Pace is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through remote services.
